# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a respectful, technically focused community.

Expected behavior includes assuming good intent, giving specific and
actionable feedback, protecting private information, and making room for
different experience levels. Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing,
credential disclosure, and malicious use of the project are not acceptable.

Maintainers may remove content or restrict participation when behavior
violates this policy. Report conduct concerns privately to the repository
owner; do not include secrets or sensitive operational data.
